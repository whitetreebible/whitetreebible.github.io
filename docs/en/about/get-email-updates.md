# Get Email Updates

Stay informed about new features, insights, and opportunities to get involved with WhiteTreeBible by signing up for our email updates through Patreon. When you join our Patreon community, you'll receive email notifications about:
- New tool releases and feature updates
- New blog posts
- New projects

Patreon will likely push for a monthly subscription, but you can choose to pledge $0 (or click Join for Free in the menu) if you just want to receive email updates without financial support.

To sign up for email updates, visit our [Patreon page](https://www.patreon.com/cw/whitetreebible) and select the option that best suits you.
